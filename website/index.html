01

Why ResearchOS

Turn fragmented AI assistance into a durable research process.

A

Research context disappears.

Transient chat history is a weak source of truth for a multi-day or multi-stage scientific project.

B

Evidence and claims drift apart.

ResearchOS keeps literature evidence, hypotheses, plans, results, and manuscript claims in explicit artifacts.

C

Automation needs boundaries.

Human gates preserve decisions that change scope, research direction, protocol, or claim boundaries.

Design principle
“A project lives in a workspace: durable files, not chat history, are the source of truth.”
02

End-to-end workflow

One research record from question to submission.

T1

Scope

Define the research question, boundaries, publication orientation, and seed material.

T2–T3.5

Evidence

Discover, verify, read, and synthesize literature into traceable research evidence.

T4

Ideation

Generate and evolve evidence-bounded research directions, then make an explicit selection.

T4.5

Formalize

Audit novelty and compile hypotheses, claims, baselines, metrics, and an executable experiment plan.

T5

Execute

Hand the accepted plan to Codex, Claude Code, or a human executor without silently changing the study.

T8–T9

Write & submit

Turn verified experimental facts into a manuscript, review package, and submission bundle.

Human decisions are first-class states, not hidden model behavior.

04

Architecture

Automation where it helps. Explicit boundaries where it matters.

⌁
Artifact-first workspace

A research project is a stateful file system.

Literature notes, idea lineage, experiment plans, executor handoffs, results, drafts, and runtime traces remain inspectable and recoverable.

workspace/ ├── literature/ ├── ideation/ ├── resources/ ├── external_executor/ ├── drafts/ └── submission/
◇
Evidence boundary

Claims do not outrun evidence.

Planned hypotheses, source-supported facts, resource leads, and verified experimental results are kept as different states.

◎
Human gates

Meaningful research choices stay visible.

ResearchOS pauses for decisions that change scope, idea selection, protocol, or claim boundaries.

↗
External execution

Use the right executor for the experiment.

Codex CLI, Claude Code, or a human executor can receive a constrained handoff and return auditable results.

≋
Research skills

Focused workflows remain reusable.

Standalone and integrated Skills cover literature intake, synthesis, ideation, writing, review, and recovery.

05

What it covers

A single runtime across the research lifecycle.

01 Literature intelligence

Discovery, deduplication, identity verification, deep reading, note cards, and synthesis.

02 Evidence-bounded ideation

Candidate research directions, comparison, evolution, novelty checks, and human selection.

03 Executable research planning

Formal hypotheses, claims, baselines, datasets, metrics, stopping rules, and protocol gates.

04 Experiment handoff

Project-specific executor Skills, resource preparation, provenance, and result validation.

05 Scientific writing

Manuscript drafting from verified facts, result-to-claim mapping, review, revision, and packaging.

06

Quick start

Three commands to start a research workspace.

Terminal 
# 1. Configure the model connection once.
python -m researchos.cli configure-llm

# 2. Create a workspace for one project.
python -m researchos.cli init-workspace \
  --workspace ./workspace/my-project \
  --project-id my-project \
  --topic "your research topic"

# 3. Start the workflow.
python -m researchos.cli run \
  --workspace ./workspace/my-project
